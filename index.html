    Machine Learning Scientific Findings & Limitations

    In our rigorous 75/25 holdout evaluation on DATATHON 2026 data, the 36-tree Random Forest achieves an MAE of 1.260, which is essentially at parity with the Mean Baseline (1.255). This occurs because synthetic quarterly incident counts are generated via a memoryless Poisson process with low location-specific autocorrelation. Operational Recommendation: Municipal dispatch decisions should rely on the transparent, auditable 5-Factor Priority Heuristic, using the ML regressor strictly as an auxiliary signal.

    SCORING ARCHITECTURE

    5-Factor Priority Index Methodology

    Auditable municipal weighting formula balancing incident history, severity, citizen input, and infrastructure context.

    Factor Weight Distribution

    Incident Volume 30% Historical reports scaled to 95th percentile ceiling
    Average Incident Severity 25% Low: 1 · Medium: 2 · High: 3 · Critical: 4 (divided by 4.0)
    Complaint Volume 20% Public complaints scaled to 95th percentile ceiling
    Open Backlog 15% Unresolved incidents + open complaints
    Street Risk Context 10% Road condition (40%), pedestrian density (30%), school/hospital (20%), density (10%)

    Auditability & Fairness Disclosures

    Why use a weighted heuristic instead of an end-to-end black-box model?

    Municipal resource allocation demands total auditability, explainability, and legal defensibility. Every point awarded corresponds directly to tangible evidence.

    How does the 95th percentile normalization prevent outlier distortion?

    Capping values at the 95th percentile prevents single extreme locations from compressing scores across the rest of the city.

    GOVERNANCE & TRANSPARENCY

    Data Integrity & Operational Disclosures

    Explicit disclosures regarding synthetic data, coordinates, and cleaned turnaround times.

    📊 Synthetic Educational Dataset

    Generated for DATATHON 2026 (1,000 locations, 10,000 incidents, 8,000 complaints). Findings demonstrate analytical capability and do not reflect real-world city infrastructure.

    📍 Simulated Geographic Coordinates

    All latitude and longitude values are simulated centroids plotted for demonstration. They must never be treated as real road hazard coordinates.

    🔀 Complaint & Incident Overlap

    Incidents and complaints are recorded independently. The queue explicitly avoids treating co-occurring records as independent physical damages.

    🛡️ Turnaround Time Cleaning

    2,874 unresolved incidents and 3,438 open complaints with populated placeholder durations were strictly excluded from completed turnaround-time calculations to prevent data contamination.

    Low: 1 · Medium: 2 · High: 3 · Critical: 4 (normalized / 4.0)
