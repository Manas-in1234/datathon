Scoring Methodology & Data Integrity Rules

Multi-pillar mathematical formulation and Stage 1 validation constraints.

Weight: 35%

1. Incident Hazard

4×Critical + 3×High + 2×Medium + 1×Low + 2×Injuries + 1×Count. Evaluates verified physical disruptions, hazard severity, and acute injuries reported by field crews.

Weight: 25%

2. Backlog Burden

2.5×Unresolved Incidents + 1.0×Open Complaints. Penalizes unaddressed hazards lingering without work-order closure to prioritize stagnant repairs.

Weight: 25%

3. Vulnerability Context

Road Condition (up to 35 pts) + School Proximity (20 pts) + Hospital Proximity (20 pts) + Pedestrians (15 pts) + Density (10 pts). Protects sensitive civilian receptors.

Weight: 15%

4. Citizen Demand

1.0×Total Complaints + 2.0×High Priority + 0.5×Normal Priority. Quantifies community dissatisfaction while remaining strictly distinct from verified physical events.

Stage 1 Quality Constraints & Integrity Assurances
  • Zero Data Modification: Source CSVs (locations.csv, incidents.csv, public_complaints.csv) remain untouched and read-only.
  • No Target Leakage: Synthetically populated resolution hours on unresolved tickets (resolved=0) are strictly excluded from turnaround scoring.
  • Complaints vs. Incidents: Public complaints represent citizen reporting sentiment and are not conflated with verified physical failures.
  • Advisory Recommendations: All suggested actions represent algorithmic triage guidance for municipal engineering crews, not confirmed structural diagnoses.
