Scoring Methodology & Mathematical Formulation

The CityFix AI Priority Engine synthesizes four normalized subscores ($S_i \in [0, 100]$) into a deterministic composite index:

Priority Score = (0.35 × S_incident) + (0.25 × S_backlog) + (0.25 × S_vulnerability) + (0.15 × S_complaint)

1. Incident Hazard (Weight: 35%)

Weighted severity sum: 4×Critical + 3×High + 2×Medium + 1×Low + 2×Injuries + 1×Count. Captures verified physical disruptions and acute safety hazards.

2. Backlog Burden (Weight: 25%)

Pending risk load: 2.5×Unresolved Incidents + 1.0×Open Complaints. Prioritizes locations where hazards have lingered without closed resolution.

3. Vulnerability Context (Weight: 25%)

Infrastructure & receptors: Road Condition (up to 35 pts) + School Proximity (20 pts) + Hospital Proximity (20 pts) + Pedestrian Activity (15 pts) + Population Density (10 pts).

4. Citizen Demand (Weight: 15%)

Community feedback load: 1.0×Total Complaints + 2.0×High Priority + 0.5×Normal Priority. Distinguishes citizen reporting urgency from physical telemetry.

Stage 1 Data Quality & Reliability Constraints

  • ⚠️ Unresolved Durations Excluded: Stage 1 audit identified that unresolved incidents (resolved=0) unconditionally contain duration values due to synthetic generator artifacts. These were strictly excluded from turnaround scoring to prevent target leakage.
  • ⚠️ Complaints vs. Incidents Distinction: Complaints reflect citizen reporting pressure and dissatisfaction; they are not assumed to have 1:1 correspondence with distinct structural failures.
  • ⚠️ Synthetic Educational Disclaimers: Coordinates and civic records are simulated for the Datathon 2026 challenge. All coordinates are labeled as synthetic and not real civic reports.
  • ⚠️ Advisory Next Actions: Suggested inspection actions are algorithmic recommendations for municipal crew triage, not confirmed structural engineering diagnoses.
